Featured project

Feedback That Changes Behavior

Self-paced e-learning module · about 12 minutes · for new people managers

New managers often avoid feedback conversations or give vague comments. This module teaches a simple, repeatable way to give specific, behavior-based feedback using the SBI model (Situation, Behavior, Impact), then lets learners practice it in a realistic conversation. The company and characters are fictional.

  • Launch the module (opens in a new tab)
  • Download the job aid (PDF) (opens in a new tab)

What is inside

  • A short hook that contrasts vague and specific feedback
  • The SBI model, with a "camera test" for telling behavior from judgment
  • A three-decision scenario where every choice gets a reply and a coach note
  • A five-question check with feedback on every answer
  • A one-page job aid managers can use before a conversation

Built with

Lumi and H5P for the interactive module, Canva for the job aid, and GitHub Pages for hosting.
